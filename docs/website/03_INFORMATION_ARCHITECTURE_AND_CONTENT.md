# Information Architecture and Content Plan

**Status:** Proposed for owner review  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 6 September 2026

## 1. Recommended primary navigation

- Shop
- The House
- The Studio
- About

The Craftiva logo returns to Home. A visible purchase/cart icon should appear only if the selected Square checkout method provides a real shared cart. Do not show nonfunctional search, account, wishlist, or cart icons.

“Journal” should be deferred until there are several worthwhile entries and an owner commitment to maintain it. “Kitchen Signature,” “Botanical Studio,” “Gather,” and “Living” can inform editorial sections and future rooms without occupying launch navigation as thin or empty pages.

## 2. Proposed sitemap

The policy pages may appear in the footer rather than primary navigation. No Craftiva order-confirmation page is required initially because Square's transaction-specific confirmation screen remains part of the checkout flow.

## 3. Homepage

### Purpose

Establish the Craftiva point of view, make the two available products obvious, and give visitors a direct path to purchase or explore.

### First viewport

- Real Craftiva photography
- Craftiva identity
- Approved current headline: **Timeless Creations for Your Space.**
- One concise supporting statement
- Primary action: **Shop Available Pieces**
- Secondary action: **Enter the House**

The hero should feature an Available product. Forthcoming or development products must not read as the object being offered for sale.

### Proposed homepage sequence

1. Hero
2. Available Now — the two products
3. The House — the three category families presented as an evolving collection
4. Forthcoming — serving tray and cutting board, subject to owner approval of public treatment
5. From the Studio — restrained development/process content
6. Our Standard — material honesty, designed for use, earned place
7. About preview — opposite-coast collaboration and design-led positioning
8. Footer

### Draft supporting statement

> Functional objects for the home, shaped by honest materials, careful use, and a belief that everyday things should be worth keeping.

Draft copy must be checked against actual materials across the assortment; avoid implying that every product is made from the same species or in the same way.

## 4. Shop

### Purpose

Provide the shortest clear route to the two products currently available for direct purchase.

### Content

- Page title and short introduction
- Two product cards with approved images
- Product name
- Price
- Concise differentiator
- Availability status
- Link to the Craftiva product page
- Purchase action placed on the product page, or directly on the card only if the Square cart behavior is clear and consistent

Do not mix Forthcoming, In Development, or Etsy-only prototypes into the normal Shop grid.

## 5. Product pages

Each Available product page should contain:

1. Product name and price
2. Image gallery
3. Plain-language value proposition
4. What is included
5. Materials and finish
6. Dimensions
7. How it works or how it installs
8. Care instructions
9. Shipping and returns summary
10. Square purchase action
11. Appropriate compatibility, capacity, or usage qualifications
12. Links back to the relevant House category

For the initial non-transactional preview, build the complete visual and factual layout but omit item 10 rather than showing a disabled or simulated purchase button. Shipping and returns may remain absent or clearly identified as unpublished internal preview content until the policies are approved. Add the real Square purchase action only in the later commerce phase.

### P01 opening direction

**Title:** Solid Wood Medal Hanger Display  
**Lead idea:** Give the miles behind every medal a lasting place in the room.  
**Supporting facts:** Solid pine; wooden medal pegs; race bib clip; designed for a substantial collection without turning the wall into clutter.

Final copy must retain qualified capacity language and use the confirmed installation method.

### P02 opening direction

**Title:** Magnetic Trivet Set – Leaf & Acorn  
**Lead idea:** A pair of solid beech trivets designed to move with compatible cookware.  
**Supporting facts:** Two-piece set; embedded magnets; silicone feet; decorative leaf and acorn forms; protects tables and counters when used as directed.

Final copy must explain cookware compatibility and should avoid implying universal magnetic attachment.

## 6. The House

### Purpose

Translate the three established catalog categories into an editorial view of the home without creating artificial inventory.

### Structure

- Introductory statement about useful objects and an evolving collection
- Display & Organization
  - Medal display as Available
- Kitchen & Table
  - Magnetic trivet set as Available
  - Serving tray and cutting board as Forthcoming, if approved
- Home & Living
  - A restrained introduction to future work; do not create a false collection

Only photographed objects should appear as objects in rooms. Empty space is preferable to invented products.

## 7. The Studio

### Purpose

Show that Craftiva is an active design studio with products at different stages, while keeping the difference between exploration and commerce unmistakable.

### Structure

- Explanation of Available, Forthcoming, and In Development
- Forthcoming work: serving tray and cutting boards
- Studio-only work: lotus and shell trivet prototypes, with an honest explanation that Craftiva is not currently bringing them to market
- In Development: wooden flower/propagation vase studies
- Process elements: sketches, drawings, material studies, prototypes, and honest design notes

Do not render an undeveloped idea to imitate finished product photography. Do not promise dates.

## 8. About

### Confirmed narrative foundation

- Craftiva is an American home-goods company founded in 2025.
- The company is design-led and works with manufacturing partners.
- It was founded across Alexandria, Virginia and Sacramento, California.
- The founders share an interest in durable, useful, restrained home objects.
- The credible story is about values, judgment, specifications, iteration, and use—not a fictional in-house workshop.

### Existing copy worth retaining as a starting point

> Founded on opposite coasts — Alexandria, Virginia and Sacramento, California — and united by the same frustration: most home goods are either commodity items or artisan pieces too precious to use. We're building the middle ground.

Owner review is required before this becomes final. For the initial launch, do not identify Jessica and Jerry by name. This can be reconsidered after Jerry discusses it with Jessica. Founder photography is therefore not required for launch.

## 9. Our Standard

Retain the three established principles, subject to product-level factual review:

- Material Honesty
- Designed for Use
- Earned Place

Avoid the old blanket statement “We use solid wood. If it looks like oak, it is oak” unless every product context supports it. Prefer a more durable principle such as:

> We describe materials plainly and choose them for the work each object needs to do. No decorative substitutions presented as something they are not.

## 10. Footer

Recommended content:

- Craftiva logo and short brand line
- Shop, The House, The Studio, About
- Shipping & Returns, Privacy, Terms
- `hello@craftiva.studio`, subject to confirmation
- Social accounts that are active and intentionally maintained
- Copyright line using the exact legal business name
- `shopcraftiva.com` need not be presented as a separate destination

## 11. Search and sharing copy

Each page needs a unique title and description. Product pages should use their real product image when shared. No fabricated review stars, scarcity messages, countdowns, or “selling fast” claims should appear.

Draft root metadata direction:

- **Title:** Craftiva — Timeless Creations for Your Space
- **Description:** Thoughtfully designed objects for the home, available directly from Craftiva.

Final wording should be reviewed after the homepage copy is approved.
