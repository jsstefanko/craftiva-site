## 1. Public-status definitions

### Available

Currently offered for normal direct purchase. Requires approved photography, a complete product page, confirmed price, active Square checkout, and inventory available for owner fulfillment.

### Forthcoming

A real product with a physical prototype or sample and a credible path to market that Craftiva has chosen to present publicly. It receives real photography and an honest description, but no price, cart control, or promised release date.

### In Development

Work Craftiva has chosen to discuss before market readiness. It is shown as studio work, process, prototype, sketch, drawing, or material study—not as a finished purchasable product. It receives no price, cart control, or release promise.

### Held / not public

A valid product or production option that Craftiva has deliberately withheld from the public site for portfolio, positioning, pricing, cannibalization, or timing reasons. It should not appear in navigation, product grids, metadata, or promotional copy.

Public status is a merchandising decision. It is not determined solely by whether a sample exists or could be ordered.

#### Website claim guardrails

- State clearly that magnetic performance depends on compatible cookware.
- Magnetic attachment works with ferrous cookware such as cast iron, enameled cast iron, carbon steel, and magnetic stainless steel.
- Aluminum, copper, glass, ceramic, and nonmagnetic stainless steel cookware will not attach magnetically; with those materials, the pieces still work as ordinary wooden trivets.
- Do not imply compatibility with all stainless steel or all cookware. A simple magnet test on the base of the cookware is the clearest consumer check.
- Position as a kitchen accessory, not a food-contact serving board.
- Do not describe the product as electrical, heating, or a novelty magnet product.
- Use the two-piece set as the normal sales configuration.
- No documented maximum surface temperature exists. Do not invent or publish a numeric heat rating, and do not use “heatproof.” Prefer “helps protect tables and counters from hot cookware when used as directed.”

## 5. Merchandising rules

1. The homepage and Shop page should lead with the two Available products.
2. A Forthcoming object must never display a price or “add to cart” control.
3. A prototype offered separately as quantity one on Etsy does not become an Available Craftiva-site product.
4. Do not link Forthcoming prototypes to Etsy.
5. Lotus and shell trivets may appear only in The Studio as work not currently being commercialized. They must not appear in Shop, The House merchandising, the homepage hero, or imagery that implies availability.
6. Wooden vases may appear only where the context clearly presents them as studio work or incidental environment—not as Available or Forthcoming merchandise.
7. The website should not create empty category pages solely to make the catalog look larger.
8. Product comparison should explain meaningful differences; price alone should not be the only distinction between related products.

## 6. Photography-status rule

A photograph may be authentic and still be unsuitable for a particular page. Before use, record every Craftiva object visible in the image and confirm that its appearance is consistent with the page's purpose and the public-status matrix.
