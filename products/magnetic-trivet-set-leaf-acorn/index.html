Use & Care

Useful across the table.

Compatibility

Magnetic attachment works with ferrous cookware such as cast iron, enameled cast iron, carbon steel, and magnetic stainless steel. Aluminum, copper, glass, ceramic, and nonmagnetic stainless steel will not attach.

Everyday use

With nonmagnetic cookware, each piece still works as an ordinary wooden trivet. Remove the trivet before returning cookware to a burner or oven. Do not use over direct flame or on a burner.

Care & packaging

Hand wash only. Not for cutting, food preparation, or dishwasher use. The set arrives in a cushioned kraft box with a thank-you card and care instructions.
